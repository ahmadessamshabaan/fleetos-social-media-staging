---

## 1. Core rule: every publishing day must tell one story

Do **not** publish isolated posts, reels, or graphics with no narrative connection.

Every active FleetOS social day must have a clear **Story Arc**:

1. **Morning Story / Teaser** — introduces the problem, question, or tension.
2. **Hero Content** — the main Reel/Post that delivers the answer, workflow, feature, or result.
3. **Follow-up Story** — closes the loop, summarizes the result, and points back to the hero content.

The audience should understand:
- what today's topic is,
- why it matters,
- what FleetOS does about it,
- and what they should watch/read next.

---

## 2. Mandatory Daily Story Arc

### A. Morning Story — Context / Hook
Purpose:
- Set up the day's question.
- Build curiosity before the main content.
- Never reveal the full answer too early.

Default format:
- One short 9:16 Story video, typically **8–10 seconds**.
- 3–4 quick scenes max.
- Clear readable text.
- Strong final line such as:
  - "Today: THE NUMBERS"
  - "Coming today"
  - "Watch what happens next"

Example:
- The trip is complete.
- Delivery done.
- Expenses captured.
- But did it make money?
- Today: THE NUMBERS.

### B. Hero Content — Main Story
Purpose:
- Deliver the full operational/product story.
- This is the main Reel or Post of the day.

Rules:
- One main idea per hero asset.
- Product flow must be based on **verified FleetOS capabilities only**.
- Use real/canonical workflow language.
- No invented features, integrations, customers, ROI, GPS, automation, AI capability, or accounting behavior.
- Prefer clear operational progression:
  **Problem → Workflow → Result**
  or
  **Input → Process → Outcome**.

### C. Follow-up Story — Result / Closure
Purpose:
- Close the day's narrative.
- Reinforce the most important number/result.
- Drive users back to the Reel/Post.

Default structure:
- Main outcome.
- One clear takeaway.
- "Watch Part X" / "See the full workflow" / equivalent CTA.

---

## 3. Content rhythm

A normal strong FleetOS publishing day should **not** be overloaded.

Default:
- 1 Morning Story
- 1 Hero Reel/Post
- 1 Follow-up Story

Do not add an extra static post unless it adds a genuinely different value.

Quality and narrative continuity are more important than post count.

---

## 4. Series continuity

When content is part of a multi-part series, each day must connect naturally to the previous part.

Example of the current series:

### Part 1
Commercial/order beginning.

### Part 2
Order → Trip → Delivery/POD → Expenses

Cliffhanger:
**"But was it profitable?"**

### Part 3
Invoice → Collection → Direct Costs → Trip Profit

Result:
- Revenue: AED 2,480
- Direct Costs: AED 1,720
- Trip Profit: AED 760

Close:
**FleetOS — From Customer to Profit.**

The next day's content must not restart randomly. It should either:
- extend the same story,
- answer the prior day's question,
- or clearly start a new named content theme.

## 6. Audio rule — mandatory

**Never rely on Instagram/Metricool native music as the only audio source for a Reel.**

The primary audio must be embedded inside the final video file before publishing.

Before approval/publishing, verify:
1. Audio stream exists.
2. Audio duration covers the full video duration.
3. No unintended silent gaps.
4. Audio level is consistent.
5. Final preview file is the same file intended for publishing.

Instagram native music may be added only as an optional enhancement when safe, but it is not the primary audio layer.

Reason:
A previous Instagram Music API selection returned only ~4.5 seconds of usable audio and did not cover the full Reel.

## 9. Platform sequence

When publishing a coordinated Instagram + Facebook Reel:

1. Verify final media and caption.
2. Publish/schedule Instagram.
3. Confirm successful publication when required.
4. Publish/schedule Facebook.
5. Confirm no duplicate queue entries.
6. Record the final live URLs when available.

For a Story Arc:
- Morning Story goes first.
- Hero content follows.
- Follow-up Story goes after the hero is live.

## 10. Content truth / product claims

FleetOS is an end-to-end fleet operations ERP/SaaS.

Canonical high-level flow:
**Customer → Quotation → Order → Trip(s) → Delivery/POD → Expenses/Direct Costs → Invoice → Collection → Trip Profit**

Never invent:
- GPS/tracking features unless verified,
- AI capability not currently live,
- customer logos/names,
- ROI percentages,
- payment integrations,
- automated reminders,
- compliance claims,
- live integrations,
- accounting behavior not verified,
- performance claims without evidence.

If unsure, verify against current FleetOS code/source of truth before publishing.

---

## 11. Copy style

FleetOS social copy should be:
- short,
- operational,
- confident,
- easy to understand,
- business-focused,
- free from unnecessary technical jargon.

Prefer:
- clear workflow arrows,
- real business language,
- strong single takeaway,
- numbers when they add meaning.

Avoid:
- generic SaaS buzzwords,
- exaggerated claims,
- feature dumping,
- long captions with no narrative,
- disconnected daily posts.
